important discoveries:

- `g_MiiLookupTable`: This global registry structure manages resident slots (typically up to $0x46$ or 70 active slots).
- The relationship system (”RelationSystem”) operates on a structured pairing matrix rather than independent actor properties.
- Triangular Matrix Indexing: Because relationship states are mutual (e.g., how Mii A relates to Mii B), the engine uses triangular matrix indexing algorithms to map pairs (A, B) into a single flat array without duplication.
- When any attribute changes, **`RelationSystem_RevalidateAllPairsForMii`** steps through every active bond tied to that resident.
- RelationSystem_CheckExistingPairConstraint checks if mii 1 and mii 2 fit, computes a combined metric or accumulation score and enforces a hard numerical boundary, If the sum exceeds 5, the engine allows exclusive relationship triggers (such as family exclusion guards, romantic pairing checks, or close-friend event gates).
- Whenever gameplay logic, save data, or packet syncs alter a Mii, these routines extract the Mii ID and instantly fire **`RelationSystem_OnMiiAttributeChanged_Revalidate`**.
- **“Type-6” Subsystem Categorization:** Special classifications (like “Type 6” handled in `ResidentRegistry_RegisterOrUpdateIncomingResident`) govern specialized social structures such as extended household registries or relative caps enforced by strict population limits
    - "Type 6" refers to an internal enumerated category or classification ID used by the relationship and resident registry subsystems to govern specialized social bonds and data arrays. it appears across the decompiled functions (such as ResidentRegistry_RegisterOrUpdateIncomingResident and ResidentRegistry_DispatchIncomingPacket)
- The game categorizes different kinds of relationships, resident entries, and connection data into distinct numerical types.
    - Lower or standard types typically handle general acquaintances or standard friendship tiers, Type 6 is reserved for a specific structural subset.
    - Think of Type 6 as a VIP guest list or a special category folder in the game's code.
    - The game uses "Type 6" to manage special relationships (like family members or roommates living together).
- Notably, “Meter” is a term that shows up in mii-to-mii relationships, which implies there is a sort of points counter that is assessed before letting something succeed.
    - Functions like `RelationSystem_CheckExistingPairConstraint` evaluate these meter scores against hard thresholds (e.g., checking if `score > 5` or if the score falls into specific ranges).
- everything i have reverse-engineered so far (RelationSystem, ResidentRegistry, and MiiManager) deals purely with abstract data structures, matrices, and ID lookups (such as looking up Mii IDs in slot arrays, checking adult flags, and evaluating index thresholds)
